## Questions for professional counsel/IPR review

- Which elements are conventional when considered alone?
- Which ordering, identity binding, or data relationships may distinguish the combination?
- Which alternatives are adequately enabled by the present disclosure?
- Which terms require narrower technical definition?
- Which project records are needed to establish chronology and contribution?
